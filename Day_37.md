💡 The insight I’m taking away:

Ownership is not the same as doing everything.

A strong owner creates clarity:

Who leads? → Who contributes? → In what order does work move? → When should it escalate?

I found that I could identify who should own a problem quite well—but designing the path the work should travel through the organization was much harder.
